# Poodle Jump 🍅

A child-friendly jumping game. Pick **Oliver** or **Elliot**, bounce up from platform to platform and dodge the cheeky tomatoes!

## How to play
- Open `index.html` in a browser (or serve the folder, e.g. `python3 -m http.server`, and visit http://localhost:8000).
- **Move:** ⬅️ ➡️ arrow keys (or A / D). On a phone or tablet, tap the left or right half of the screen, or tilt the device.
- **Tomatoes:** bumping into one costs a ❤️ (you have 3). Land on top of one to **squish** it for +50!
- **Stars:** +25 bonus points.
- **Platforms:** green = normal, blue = moving, white clouds puff away after one bounce, red springs launch you extra high.
- Fall off the bottom and it's game over. Best scores are remembered for each player.

## Assets
`assets/oliver.png` and `assets/elliot.png` are the boys' photos with the background removed (BiRefNet portrait model via `rembg`).
